# Product

NOVRIX is a crypto sentiment workspace. It reads on-chain and macro indicators as one calm surface, and publishes two editorial formats: Our View and Deep Dive.

## Who it is for

Operators and researchers who want to read market structure without a trading interface, an account, or a feed of recycled headlines.

## Truth constraints

- No public accounts, sign-in, paywall, ads, or analytics.
- Sentiment requires a viewport of 1024px or wider; editorial surfaces work from 375px up.
- Data is cached at the edge and falls back to a live fetch when stale.
- Editorial content lives in Cloudflare D1 and is served by `/api/posts`.
- The site is a static export; only `/api/*` is dynamic.

## Voice

Direct, specific, unhurried. No growth language, no invented numbers, no fake activity. State limits as plainly as capabilities.
