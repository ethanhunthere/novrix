# AI Crawler Access Policy

This file defines access rules for AI crawlers, LLM training bots, and search agents.

## Allowed Crawlers

The following AI crawlers are permitted to access all public content on novrix.io:

- GPTBot (OpenAI)
- ChatGPT-User (OpenAI)
- Claude-Web (Anthropic)
- anthropic-ai (Anthropic)
- PerplexityBot (Perplexity)
- Google-Extended (Google)
- Applebot-Extended (Apple)
- BingPreview (Microsoft)
- OAI-SearchBot (OpenAI)

## Access Rules

- **Allow**: All public pages and content
- **Disallow**: /admin/ and /api/auth/ (sensitive/administrative paths)
- **Crawl-delay**: None (respect standard rate limits)
