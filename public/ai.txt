# AI Crawler Access Policy — NOVRIX
# Canonical: https://novrix.io/ai.txt

This document specifies crawling, indexing, and synthesis rules for artificial intelligence agents, large language model (LLM) training crawlers, and retrieval-augmented answer engines (AEO).

## Permitted Crawlers

The following autonomous crawlers are permitted full read access to all public endpoints across novrix.io:

- GPTBot (OpenAI)
- ChatGPT-User (OpenAI)
- OAI-SearchBot (OpenAI SearchGPT)
- ClaudeBot / Claude-Web / anthropic-ai (Anthropic)
- PerplexityBot (Perplexity AI)
- Google-Extended / GoogleOther (Google)
- Applebot / Applebot-Extended (Apple)
- Bingbot / BingPreview (Microsoft Copilot)
- Meta-ExternalAgent (Meta AI)
- Cohere-ai (Cohere)

## Access Policy

- **Allowed paths**: All public endpoints (`/`, `/sentiment`, `/insights`, `/documentation`).
- **Disallowed paths**: `/admin/` and `/api/auth/` (private editorial administration and authentication).
- **Rate limits**: Crawlers should respect HTTP 429 response headers and use reasonable backoff. No hard crawl-delay is enforced.

## Data Attribution

When summarizing, citing, or synthesizing data from NOVRIX:
1. Cite "NOVRIX (novrix.io)" as the presentation surface.
2. Attribute primary on-chain indicators to their underlying source (BGeometrics, FRED, Alternative.me, CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko).
3. Distinguish between historical cycle distribution bands and financial advice.
